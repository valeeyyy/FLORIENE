The Story of Floriene

A little idea born from free time, a love for flowers, and a simple concern - that real flowers fade too soon.

How It Started

A Holiday, A Hook, and A Dream 🧶

Floriene was born during the semester break between Semester 1 and Semester 2 - a stretch of free time that could have been spent doing nothing. But for the founders, nganggur (idling around) wasn't really an option.

With colorful pipe cleaners on the table, one flower became two, two became ten, and ten became a small collection that friends started noticing and asking about.

"I just wanted to make something beautiful with my hands during the break. I never expected it would turn into something people actually wanted to buy."

What started as a holiday hobby quickly became a passion project - and eventually, Floriene was born.

The Concern

Real Flowers Are Beautiful - But They Don't Last 🥀

We've all been there: a beautiful bouquet on the table, fresh and vibrant on day one. By day three, petals begin to fall. By the end of the week, it's gone.

The founders noticed this everyday concern in the people around them - friends who wanted to give flowers but felt sad that they would wilt so quickly, and recipients who wished their gift could last longer.

"Flowers are such a meaningful gift, but watching them wilt so fast always felt a little heartbreaking. I thought - what if I could make flowers that stay beautiful, forever?"

That thought sparked everything. Pipe cleaner flowers don't wilt. They don't need water. They never shed petals. They sit on a shelf, hang on a bag, or rest in a vase - forever as beautiful as the day they were made.

Loyalty Program

Digital + Physical Loyalty Card 🎀

Floriene offers two complementary ways to track your loyalty points - the digital card in your account and the physical stamp card. Both work together so your points are never lost.

📱
Digital Loyalty Card
Your loyalty points are automatically tracked in the Floriene system every time your order is marked as completed by admin. You can always check your point balance anytime through your Profile page - no need to bring anything, no risk of losing it.

Best for: Instant tracking, never forgetting how many points you have, and redeeming rewards hassle-free.
💳
Physical Loyalty Card
The physical card is a tangible stamp card that gets stamped each time you make a purchase at campus. It's a fun collectible that you can hold, carry, and show off - and it serves as a backup verification alongside the digital system.

Best for: Campus transactions, showing proof of your loyalty in person, and collecting as a cute Floriene keepsake!
🌸 Both cards track the same loyalty program. Digital keeps you updated automatically, physical gives you a fun collectible - together, they make sure your rewards are always safe!
